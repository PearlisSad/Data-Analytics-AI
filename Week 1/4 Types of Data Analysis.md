# Descriptive(NOT ANALYSIS)
- Summarising past data to understand what has happened in a business scenario
- Uses historucal data to identify trends and patterns
# Diagnostic
- "Why did it happen?"
- uses drilldown, data discovery, correlation and causality determination to understand underlying causes of observed outcomes
# Predictive
- Leveraging statistical models and machine learning techniques, predictive analytics
- Used in risk management, marketing
# Prescriptive
- Suggests actions taken for desired outcomes and prevent undesirable outcomes
# Cognitive
- Mimics human brain's ability to learn, reason and make decision
- uses data mining, pattern recognition and natural language processing
