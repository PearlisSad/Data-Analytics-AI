
Narrative Comparison

- **Descriptive statistics**: numerical summary (means, ranges).

- **Descriptive analytics**: highlights trends and historical behaviour.

- **Diagnostic analytics**: explains causes of changes (crises, shocks).

- **Predictive analytics**: forecasts likely future paths.

- **Prescriptive analytics**: suggests actions or policies.

- **Cognitive analytics**: uses AI/advanced models to augment insights.

 Comparison Table

|Analytics Type|Question Answered|Example from UK Data|
|---|---|---|
|Descriptive Statistics|What are the averages/ranges?|GDP avg £490bn, CPI avg 2.4%|
|Descriptive Analytics|What happened?|GDP fell in 2009 and 2020|
|Diagnostic Analytics|Why did it happen?|Crises drove GDP and investment down|
|Predictive Analytics|What will happen?|Inflation may decline, rates may stabilise|
|Prescriptive Analytics|What should be done?|Tighten policy in inflation, loosen in recessions|
|Cognitive Analytics|How can AI help?|Detect downturns, simulate policy effects|
